Now

I work across DroneDeploy's processing pipeline, the tile servers behind its maps, and the web app that shows them. Most days that means PROJ pipelines, datums, and making big rasters load fast.

I also rebuilt its measurement system: lengths, areas, volumes, and cut/fill, computed over triangulated surfaces. Try it on the map above with the Measure button.

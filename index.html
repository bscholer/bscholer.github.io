Now

I work across DroneDeploy's processing pipeline, the tile servers behind its maps, and the web app that shows them. Most days that means GDAL, coordinate systems, and making big rasters load fast.

Before that I built GIS and traffic-data tools for state DOTs at HNTB and Purdue's Joint Transportation Research Program. Purdue CIT, class of 2020. FAA Part 107 pilot.
